experience: supports its point mainly with something the writer personally did, built, used, measured or saw happen
argument: supports a claim about the topic with at least one reason a reader could check or dispute on its own (a fact, a number, a mechanism, a source, an outside example) rather than the writer's own experience
assertion: takes a position on the topic (a verdict, a prediction, an opinion, a sarcastic jab at the subject) and offers nothing to support it beyond the claim itself
chatter: makes no claim about the topic at all: thanks, praise or jabs aimed at a person or their project, clarifying questions, concessions, bare link drops, and talk about the thread itself
